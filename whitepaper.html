Project Overview

JIPUNG Coin (JIP) is a new digital cryptocurrency project that is being developed with the goal of providing a simple and accessible way to transfer digital value.

The project has a fixed maximum supply of 59,000,000 JIP. This supply limit is part of the project's initial design and is intended to provide a clear and predictable supply structure.

JIPUNG Coin aims to gradually build an open and transparent ecosystem where users may participate in digital transactions and community-driven activities.

As a new project, JIPUNG Coin is still under development. The project is working to establish its technology, ecosystem, and community step by step, while exploring practical ways for JIP to be used in the future. The project does not guarantee future value, adoption, or success.
